# Retail Sales EDA - Oasis Infobyte

## Project Overview
Exploratory Data Analysis on retail sales dataset to uncover patterns, customer behavior trends, and actionable business insights.

## Tasks Completed
- ✅ Data loading & initial inspection
- ✅ Descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, std)
- ✅ Time series analysis (monthly & quarterly trends)
- ✅ Customer demographics (age groups, gender)
- ✅ Product analysis (revenue by category)
- ✅ Correlation heatmap
- ✅ Additional visualization (boxplot - spending by age)
- ✅ Business recommendations

## Tech Stack
- **Python** (pandas, numpy)
- **Visualization** (matplotlib, seaborn)
- **Google Colab** 

## Key Insights
| Insight | Finding |
|---------|---------|
| Top Category | Electronics (156,905, 34.4%) |
| Best Month | May 2023 |
| Best Quarter | Q4 2023 |
| Largest Age Group | 45-54 (225 customers) |
| Highest Spenders | 55-64 age group |
| Key Correlation | Price vs Revenue (0.85) |

## Recommendations
1. **Product**: Expand Electronics, bundle accessories
2. **Customers**: Loyalty program for 45-54 & 55-64
3. **Seasonal**: Increase marketing before May & Q4
4. **Pricing**: Premium pricing works, volume discounts
